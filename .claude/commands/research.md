Research a historical topic for a new History Pulse video.

Topic or theme given by the human: $ARGUMENTS

Do the following:

1. Use web search to investigate the topic. If it's broad, propose 3–5 specific historical
   event angles within it. For each angle, give:
   - A one-line summary of the event
   - The **modern relevance hook**: why this matters or resonates right now (anniversary date
     against today's actual date, current news parallel, pop culture tie-in, or "you didn't
     know this" surprise factor)
   - A rough sense of search demand / how well similar topics tend to perform (reason about
     this from search results, don't fabricate numbers)
2. Verify key facts, dates, and figures against multiple sources. Flag anything historians
   dispute.
3. Recommend which single angle to move forward with, and propose a URL-safe slug for it
   (lowercase, hyphenated, e.g. `1914-july-crisis`).
4. Write your findings to `content/01_research/<slug>/notes.md`, including:
   - The chosen angle and why
   - Key facts with source notes (not verbatim quotes — your own summary)
   - A short bibliography of source links
   - Suggested public-domain/royalty-free image or map sources for this topic (Wikimedia
     Commons, Library of Congress, Internet Archive, etc.)
5. End by asking the human: "Research is saved for `<slug>`. Want me to move to `/script
   <slug>`, or should I dig into a different angle first?"

Do not write the narration script yet — that's a separate step.
