**Target length:** 75–85s · **Record:** your reaction + your own backing footage · **Code:** once, ~0:55

---

## The idea
**Stitch someone else's Fortnite clip, react to it, then give your own take backed by your own footage.**

Lowest-effort format of the week: use it when you have nothing else. It only works with a REAL clip
from a creator who has Stitch/Duet enabled. The script below is the structure; the clip, the take
and the footage are yours.

## What is verified and what is NOT
- **Verified (season guides, checked 2026-10-04):** the season is Chapter 7 Season 4 "Override"
  (Aug 20 – Oct 31, 2026). Guides list the Pump Shotgun, Drum Gun, Ranger Assault Rifle and
  Tactical Pistol in this season's loot pool, and mention Override Consoles, movement Sprites and the
  1-Up Token.
- **NOT verified — placeholders until you pick the clip:** which creator, which clip, and what happens
  in it. Never describe a clip you haven't watched. Do not quote their words; paraphrase your reaction.
- **Rules for this format:** use TikTok's own Stitch/Duet on a creator who has it enabled. Don't
  re-upload their clip as your own post — that is the unoriginal-content rule. Don't use anything
  that attacks the creator; disagree with the play, not the person.

## Shot list (record before editing)

1. Their clip, 3–5 seconds, via Stitch (the most arguable moment)
2. You on camera / voiceover reacting, unscripted first take
3. Your own footage backing your take (a clip of you doing it differently)
4. Your own footage of how you'd have played it
5. 3–4 seconds of you reacting at the end
