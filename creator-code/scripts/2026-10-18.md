## The idea
**Stitch one arguable Fortnite clip from another creator, react to it, then give your real take with your own footage.**

Lowest-effort format of the week. Use TikTok's own Stitch/Duet feature on a public clip whose creator
left Stitch on: that is the licensed way to use someone else's video. Do NOT download and re-upload
their clip as your own post, and never cut it longer than the 3–5 seconds the format shows.

## Picking the clip
- Pick the most *arguable* moment: a rotation, a loot call, a build fight, a "this is broken" claim.
- Prefer a claim you can test or disprove in your own game this week.
- Skip anything about a real person's private life, harassment, or cheating accusations.
  Argue the play, never the player.

## What is verified and what is NOT
- **Verified (web search 2026-10-18; launch-era coverage only, no October patch notes found):** Chapter 7
  Season 4 "Override" launched Aug 20, 2026, with a Gaming Legends theme (Sonic and other game crossovers).
  Earlier checks in this repo place the season end at Oct 31.
- **NOT verified:** anything about the clip you pick (who made it, what actually happened, whether the
  claim is true), any current meta or patch detail, and any stat. Say only what you can show on screen
  from your own footage. If your take depends on a game rule, check it in game first.
- Never state or imply the creator code gives a discount. It doesn't.

## Shot list
1. Their clip (3–5 s, via Stitch) — the arguable moment
2. You on camera, immediate reaction (about 15 s)
3. Your own footage testing/backing your take (about 40 s)
4. You on camera, where you would have played it differently (about 15 s)
