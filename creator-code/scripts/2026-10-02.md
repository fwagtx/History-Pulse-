  2026). The Pump Shotgun, Ranger Assault Rifle, Assault Rifle, Drum Gun, Minigun and Tactical
  Pistol are all in this season's loot pool (unvaulted at launch). Several tier lists put the
  Pump Shotgun and Drum Gun in their top tiers and the Assault Rifle, Tactical Pistol and Minigun
  a step below, with Midas' Masterpiece and the Striker AR on top. Tier lists disagree with each
  other, which is the point of the video.
- **NOT verified — only say these on camera if you've seen them in your own games:** exact damage
  numbers (one guide quotes 205 headshot damage for a Legendary Pump — don't repeat it without
  checking in game), spawn rates, and any claim about SMGs (sources disagree on whether any are in the pool).
- **The ranking itself is your opinion.** Don't copy a tier list. Rank what you actually used.
- Per the Friday rule: put one gun *slightly* out of place on purpose, and say why. Don't be obnoxious.
