# What's working: the numbers behind the lineup

A running record of what the analytics say and what was changed because of it, newest first.
Numbers come from Metricool (brand 7066444 only). Each weekly review adds a section.

---

## 2026-11-22: why YouTube subscribers grew so slowly

**Why:** the owner asked why the channel had stayed under 100 subscribers for so long.

### The numbers

- **Growth so far:**
  - 0 to 74 in the first 3½ weeks (Sep 23 – Oct 17).
  - Stuck at 74–79 for three weeks (Oct 17 – Nov 7).
  - 79 to **101** since Nov 8, when the Pirate Shop Access videos started. It passed 100 on
    Nov 21.
- **Subscribers follow views.** In early October daily views were 2,000–4,000. In late
  October they fell to a few hundred a day, and the Oct 31 – Nov 4 outage posted nothing.
  Growth stopped in both stretches.
- **Conversion:** about 1 new subscriber per 770 views overall. It was 1 per 3,200 in the
  stall and about 1 per 1,200 since Nov 8.
- **Where views came from** (last 30 days, about 33,000):

  | Source | Share |
  |---|---|
  | Shorts feed (people swiping) | 78% |
  | YouTube search | 19% |
  | Channel page | 1.4% |
  | Subscribers' feeds | 0.5% |

  The subscribers barely watch, and most views come from swipers who watch 7–26 seconds.
- **Audience age:** 13–17 29%, 18–24 9%, 25–34 23%, 35–44 23%, 45+ 15%.
- **Nothing asked anyone to subscribe.** No Short said "subscribe" or "follow" on screen,
  in the voice or in the description. The only ask was the creator code, at the very end
  (60+ seconds in), which most viewers never reach.
- The channel page couldn't be checked from here (YouTube is blocked in this workspace).

### Changed

- Every description now has "🔔 Follow for the Fortnite Item Shop every day".
- The voiced Pirate Shop Access videos end with "Follow for tomorrow's shop / countdown /
  odds!" before "use code bad".

### For the owner (YouTube Studio, only they can do it)

1. **Name the channel for what it is**, e.g. "BAD · Daily Fortnite Item Shop", and keep the
   @handle. A channel called after a creator code reads like an ad.
2. **Banner and description that promise something:** the Item Shop every day, Pirate
   Shop Access and news, rarest-skin countdowns, a long video every Friday, plus
   "Support-A-Creator code BAD #EpicPartner".
3. **Verify the channel** with a phone number, so the Friday video's custom thumbnail shows.
4. **Three playlists** (Fortnite Item Shop Today, Pirate Shop Access, Rarest Skins), with
   the channel home page showing them. Search brings 19% of views, and playlists keep
   those viewers watching.
5. **Pin a comment** on the best videos: "New Item Shop video every day, subscribe so you
   don't miss tomorrow's 🔔".

### Content ideas waiting on the owner

- **Shorter Shorts (30–45 s) with the payoff in the first second.** Reach depends on how
  much of each video people watch.
- **After Dec 5, keep a "news" slot for the next big thing** (Winterfest, the new season,
  collabs). Trending topics brought the November growth.

## 2026-11-20: first review (long videos cut to one a week)

**Why:** the owner said there were far too many long YouTube videos, half of them under 10
views.

### YouTube long videos (Sep 30 – Nov 17, 28 videos)

- Median about 11 views. 13 of the 28 got 10 views or fewer.
- Average view duration was 30–70 seconds on 8–10 minute videos, so most viewers left in the
  first minute.
- Best: *The 30 Rarest Chapter 1, Season 6 Skins: Where Are They Now?* (127),
  *The 30 Rarest Icon Series Skins* (93 on Nov 13, 36 on Oct 2), *This Week in the Item
  Shop* (45 and 44).
- Median views by day: **Friday 22** (the rarest Icon Series / Marvel / DC / Star Wars /
  Gaming Legends skins), Thursday about 17, Tuesday and Wednesday 9, Monday 5.
- **Changed:** one a week, on Friday at 3 PM Central (`long_days` in `lineup.json`).

### YouTube Shorts (Nov 9–18)

- Best: Pirate Shop Access *Your Odds After 14 Days* (1,229), *5 Things to Know* (1,209),
  *The Oldest Items in Today's Shop* on Nov 13 (1,007), *The Most Common Skins* (988),
  *How Rare Is Pirate Shop Access* (979), *The Most Common Skins This Year* (966),
  *The Most Common Pickaxes* (899), *The One Piece Items to Claim* (876).
- Weak: *Back in the Shop After N Days* (17–110). It repeated the same item for up to five
  days (Nov 11–15 were all "1,144 days"). The 7 PM "gone the longest" and "rarest"
  countdowns often got under 25.
- **Changed:** *Back After* now features only items that came back that day.
- Channel: 74 to 98 subscribers in 30 days. Daily views reached 1,600–3,200 in mid-November,
  mostly from the Pirate Shop Access Shorts.

### TikTok (Nov 5–19)

- About 10,160 followers, gaining 1–5 a day. About 2,000–3,600 views a day.
- Average views by slot (Central time):

  | Slot | Average views |
  |---|---|
  | 11:00 AM Item Shop | about 800 (best: 2,240) |
  | 8:00 AM countdown | about 450 (only 116–222 this week) |
  | 9:30 AM Pirate grabs | about 390 |
  | 4:00 PM Back After | about 350 |
  | 7:00 PM countdown | about 330 (falling) |
  | 2:30 PM Pirate spotlight | about 260 |
  | 1:00 PM OG Check | about 245 |
  | 8:00 PM Pirate evening | about 170 |

- **Average watch time is 4–10 seconds** on 62–90 second videos. Most viewers swipe away
  in the first few seconds. This is the biggest lever.

### Ideas waiting on the owner

1. **Hook harder in the first 2 seconds:** put the payoff (the #1 item, the big number) on
   frame 0 and in the first spoken line.
2. **Test shorter Shorts (30–45 s) on one slot for a week.** TikTok favours videos people
   finish. The 62-second minimum only matters for TikTok's Creator Rewards, which needs
   100,000 views in 30 days (we're at about 70,000).
3. **The 8 PM Pirate Shop video is the weakest on TikTok** (about 170 views), but the
   evening Pirate videos are among the best on YouTube. Keep it until Dec 5, then don't
   replace that slot.
